# 🌅 What is Watchtower?

In today’s geopolitical climate, nations are in a race to be leaders in Space among other arenas like Artificial Intelligence, Quantum computing, Nuclear, etc. as they double down on space assets for defense and data sovereignty.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (38).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Of the $74 billion in startup space investment between 2016-2025, just 4%—about $3 billion—came from private credit alone. While that’s small for traditional markets standards, it represents a significant opportunity for crypto and blockchain-based lending. As the space sector evolves from Gen 1 leaders to new Gen 3 players, the biggest names now lean on private credit, transforming how space projects are financed. Over $500 million in credit have powered firms like SpaceX, Rocket Lab and AST SpaceMobile not counting prime contractors like Boeing.
